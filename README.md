# Bank of Baroda Banking Performance Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive banking performance dashboard designed to help bank executive management monitor financial health, credit portfolio growth, asset quality, branch performance, and customer segment metrics.

## Business Objective

The objective of this project is to build an executive-level banking dashboard that consolidates critical financial indicators into a single actionable view. It enables stakeholders to assess liquidity, monitor asset quality (NPAs), evaluate loan portfolio compositions, and track top-performing state branches and customer growth.

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Executive Summary Cards
Provides top-level metrics on Total Deposits, Advances, Active Customer Count, Gross NPA %, Net Profit, and CASA Ratio with YoY performance indicators.

### 2. Finance Performance Trend
Tracks monthly growth from April 2025 to December 2025 for Closing Deposits, Closing Advances, and Net Profit trajectory.

### 3. Deposits vs. Advances Ratio
Visualizes the overall business volume split (₹14,122 Cr total business: 58% Deposits vs. 42% Advances).

### 4. Branch Performance by State
Ranks branch operations across key Indian states (Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Uttar Pradesh, and Others) by branch count and average performance percentage.

### 5. Loan Portfolio Composition
Analyzes loan disbursements across key products including Home Loans (32.2%), Vehicle Loans (14.8%), Personal Loans (14.1%), MSME Loans (11.9%), Agriculture Loans (10.1%), Business Loans (9.0%), and Others.

### 6. Top 5 Branches by Business Volume
Highlights leading regional branches by business volume generated (Mumbai Main, Bengaluru Main, Chennai T.N., Pune FC Road, and Ahmedabad).

### 7. Customer Growth & Segment Mix
Monitors active vs. new customer growth trends alongside segment distribution across Retail, MSME, and Corporate customers.

### 8. Interactive Slicers
Enables dynamic filtering across customizable date ranges and individual branch locations.

## Business Questions Addressed

- How are closing deposits and advances trending month-over-month?
- What is the current deposit-to-advance balance across the bank's total business?
- How effectively is the bank managing asset quality (Gross NPA improvement)?
- Which loan categories drive the largest share of total advances?
- Which state branches generate the highest overall business volume?
- What is the growth rate of active customers versus newly acquired customers?

## How to Explore the Dashboard

1. Download the Power BI report (`.pbix`) file from the repository.
2. Open the report using Microsoft Power BI Desktop.
3. Update the data source connections/file paths if prompted.
4. Click **Refresh** to load the data model.
5. Use the top slicers to filter performance by **Date Range** and **Branch**.
